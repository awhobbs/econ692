\begin{frame}{Common Estimation Pitfalls}
  \small
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Wrong standard errors} ---
      Panel data $\Rightarrow$ cluster at the treatment level. \texttt{feols(..., cluster = \textasciitilde{}state)} not \texttt{vcov = "HC1"}

    \item \textbf{Fixed effects absorb your treatment} ---
      If treatment only varies by year, year FE kill it. You need \textbf{cross-sectional variation}.

    \item \textbf{Collinearity warnings} ---
      A variable is a linear combination of your FE. Drop it or rethink the spec.

    \item \textbf{``No variation in treatment''} ---
      Check: is the treatment variable coded correctly? Are the right units treated?

    \item \textbf{Sample size drops dramatically} ---
      Merges or missing values silently dropping observations. Check \texttt{nobs()}.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{frame}
