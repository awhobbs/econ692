# MSAE Capstone Project Showcase — Past Projects

## Spring 2021

| # | Student | Title | Description | Methods/Tools |
|---|---------|-------|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | Michael Emanuel | Effects of a Minimum Wage on Low-Wage Labor Markets in Contiguous Counties | Examines how state-level minimum wage differences affect earnings and employment for teenagers and restaurant workers in border county pairs across the US. | RDD, panel OLS, county-pair FE, McCrary density test, BLS/CPS data |

Plantin | The Economic Impact of FDI on Colombia's Two Major Cities: Bogota and Medellin | Estimates the effect of FDI inflows on GDP per capita with a focus on whether tertiary education mediates the FDI-growth relationship. | VAR/ARIMA, Granger causality, SEM mediation analysis, panel FE, World Bank data |

| 5 | Bryan Isaac | Absentee and All Cash Purchases in the San Francisco Housing Market | Analyzes whether absentee buyers and all-cash purchasers pay systematically different prices for residential properties in San Francisco. | OLS log-linear hedonic regression, neighborhood interaction effects, CoreLogic data |
| 6 | Mohammed Alrashidan | Evaluating Fintech Policy in Saudi Arabia | Uses DiD to evaluate whether Saudi Arabia's fintech regulatory initiatives affected point-of-sale transaction volumes and business revenues. | Difference-in-Differences, panel OLS, SAMA data (1995–2021) |
| 7 | Nikki Nguyen | Exploring the Causal Link Between Cannabis Access and Hard Drug Abuse | Estimates the causal effect of Colorado's 2012 cannabis legalization on hard drug overdose deaths using 9 county-level synthetic controls. | Synthetic Control Method, county-level panel data (2001–2019) |
| 8 | Noeru Shimmoto | Remote Work and Productivity | Estimates the average treatment effect of remote work on self-reported productivity using propensity score matching with a UK web survey (n=14,123). | Propensity Score Matching, logistic regression, OLS on matched sample |
